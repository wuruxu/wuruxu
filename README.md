## 100-word intro

I am wuruxu, a systems-minded developer who enjoys turning low-level parts into usable tools. My work moves between Java, C++, Chromium, Linux, OpenWrt, Debian, WireGuard, and IPv6, with a bias toward practical engineering over shiny abstractions. I like browsers, networks, build pipelines, and the quiet details that make software reliable. Projects such as Kiwi Next reflect how I approach code: keep it close to upstream, automate the boring paths, debug patiently, and ship things people can actually run. I am always exploring better ways to connect devices, kernels, browsers, and users through clean, understandable, open-source work, every day with care.
